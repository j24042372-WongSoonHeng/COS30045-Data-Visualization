Frequently Asked Questions

How is the kWh/year calculated?

The kWh/year is calculated based on standard testing procedures under typical usage conditions in Australian homes over a 365-day period.

What does the star rating mean?

The star rating represents the appliance's energy efficiency compared to other models of the same size and capacity. More stars mean more efficiency.

How can I reduce my standby power?

You can reduce standby power by unplugging appliances when not in use or using smart power strips that cut power to peripheral devices.
