The Hidden Cost of Choosing a TV

Most everyday consumers have little visibility into where their electricity bills really come from. While the heaviest energy hogs in typical Australian households are usually electric stovetops, electric ovens, and clothes dryers, televisions are fixtures in almost every living room. As households constantly ask themselves "should we upgrade to a bigger screen?", choosing the wrong size or panel technology can quietly send energy bills soaring.

Who Is This Story For?

This data narrative is crafted for everyday Australian consumers, home buyers, and families who are currently shopping for a new television or looking to demystify their quarterly power bills. Whether you are weighing an upgrade from 55 inches to 75 inches or deciding between LED and OLED, this story reveals what the energy numbers mean in practice.

Key Takeaway: Size is the Deciding Factor

"If your goal is to keep household electricity bills in check, don't look at picture quality alone — screen size is the single biggest deciding factor."

Before purchasing, determine the optimal viewing distance for your room. A well-proportioned screen prevents unnecessary power drain every hour it remains switched on.
