Empowering Australian Consumers Through Energy Transparency

Rising electricity bills and growing awareness of climate impact have made it more important than ever for households to understand how their appliances consume energy. We are dedicated to providing clear, authoritative data on appliance energy efficiency to help you make informed decisions.

What This Site Covers

From everyday kitchen appliances to televisions and air conditioners, this site breaks down typical energy usage so you can see where your electricity actually goes.

Why We Built This

Electricity bills keep climbing, but most people have no idea which appliances are actually driving up their usage. This site puts that information in one simple place.

Where the Numbers Come From

The figures shown across this site are based on typical energy consumption ranges reported for Australian households — for example, standard fridge/freezer usage and average split-system air conditioner running costs. Individual results will vary by model, size, and usage habits.
